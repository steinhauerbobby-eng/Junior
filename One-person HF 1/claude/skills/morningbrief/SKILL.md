Run the **Macro Researcher** and **Catalyst Monitor** agents in parallel. Then synthesize their outputs into the following structured morning briefing:

---

## MORNING BRIEF — {today's date}

### 1. Macro Regime
[One sentence characterizing the current macro environment from Macro Researcher]

### 2. Top Headlines
[3-5 most important headlines from Catalyst Monitor, each with a 1-2 sentence summary and sentiment label]

### 3. Recent Indicator Results
[Any economic data released in the past 48 hours — result vs. estimate, and what it means for the macro picture. Pull from Macro Researcher output.]

### 4. Macro Bull Case
[One paragraph: what scenario leads to a risk-on, growth-positive outcome from here]

### 5. Macro Bear Case
[One paragraph: what scenario leads to a risk-off, growth-negative outcome from here]

### 6. Key Events This Week
[Upcoming economic prints, Fed speakers, earnings for portfolio/watchlist names — from Catalyst Monitor macro calendar]

Keep the total briefing scannable — the operator reads this before markets open. Prioritize signal over completeness.
