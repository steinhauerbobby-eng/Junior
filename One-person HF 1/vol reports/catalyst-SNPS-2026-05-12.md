## Trade Structuring Notes

**Setup summary:** Expensive straddle (~2.4x ex-outlier), DOWN bias (6/8 quarters), consistent sells-off on guidance misses even with EPS beats, uniform put UOA, max pain just below current price. The guidance-driven, bearish-leaning setup argues for directional downside expression rather than neutral premium selling.

**Why not a short strangle / iron condor?**
The uniform put UOA (3/3 prints downside) is a caution flag for naked put selling specifically. Selling puts into active institutional put buying reduces the premium-selling edge and increases the risk of being on the wrong side of an informed flow. A defined-risk structure is preferable.

**Potential structures:**

1. **Put spread — primary (directional, defined risk):**
   Buy May 29 $500P / Sell May 29 $470P — captures the max pain gravity (-2.6%) and historical DOWN bias. Profits on any modest selloff; max loss is the spread premium. Aligns with UOA, history, and straddle put > call.

2. **Call ratio spread (sell upside premium, fund downside):**
   Buy 1x May 29 $530C / Sell 2x May 29 $560C — monetizes the expensive upside IV (calls less demanded than puts) while retaining a call for a positive guidance surprise. Risk: stock rallies past $560 (would be very unusual for SNPS earnings history).

3. **Avoid long straddle:** 2.4x effective ratio makes this structurally expensive. SNPS would need to move ~11% to profit; only happened once (May 2025: -11.1%) in normal earnings conditions.

4. **Avoid naked short puts:** UOA is uniformly bearish — selling puts into institutional put demand is fighting the flow with no edge.

**Key risk to bear thesis:** A significant guidance raise (AI design tool demand acceleration, SNPS revenue mix improving from slower EDA refresh cycles) could produce an outsized upside move. The 2024-05-22 quarter (+2.6% on +1.6% surprise) is the only clean upside case — modest and well within the straddle range.
