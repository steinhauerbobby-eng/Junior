## Trade Structuring Notes

**Key context — the beats-that-sell dynamic:** Three of the last five reports were DOWN despite large EPS beats (+10.6%, +19.9%, +14.9% EPS surprise). This happened post-July 2024 Falcon outage — the market was discounting ARR churn and customer retention risk through the beats. The last two quarters (Dec 2025, Mar 2026) were both UP on modest beats, suggesting the post-outage overhang has cleared and the stock is now moving directionally with beats. This improves the bull case into June.

**Potential structures:**

1. **Short strangle (primary):** Sell Jun $465P / $575C — collects premium on both wings, profitable if stock stays within ±~10% of current price. Historical move (5% avg) suggests 6 of 8 quarters would have been profitable at these strikes. Risk: the one 10.9% move (Jun 2024) would have breached the call side at $575.
   
2. **Iron condor (defined risk):** Buy Jun $440P / Sell Jun $465P / Sell Jun $575C / Buy Jun $600C — limits max loss, captures the premium-selling edge without naked short tail risk.

3. **Put spread (directional short):** Jun $515P / $480P — captures the max pain gravitational pull toward $450 while defining risk. Works if stock disappoints or trades flat.

4. **Avoid long straddle:** 2.57x ratio makes premium buying structurally disadvantaged. Needs 12.9% move; only happened once in 8 quarters.

**Primary risk to short-premium thesis:** An undisclosed binary catalyst (acquisition, major platform announcement, or significant NRR/ARR miss outside consensus) could produce the 10–15% move the straddle is pricing. At 26 DTE, there's meaningful time for news to develop.
